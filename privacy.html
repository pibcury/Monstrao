Monster Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

The short version

  • Monster is a small, non-commercial calorie, macro, burn and weight tracker shared with invited friends.
  • We use your information only to run the app for you. We don't sell it, show ads, or share it for marketing.
  • If you connect Apple Health, we receive a few numbers per day: your Active Energy and step count, and — only if you turn on “Weight from Apple Health” — your first weigh-in of the day and its time. You can disconnect at any time.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and everything in it whenever you like.

Who we are

Monster (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a personal project operated by Pedro Cury for himself and people he invites. It is not a company product and is not offered commercially. Questions about this policy or your data can go to pibcury@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address and the name you sign up with. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see or store it.
  • What you log: foods and drinks (descriptions, calories, protein, carbs and fat, an optional meal tag such as “Lunch”, and when each was logged), calories burned, workouts you log (activity, duration and effort), body weight readings, and profile and goal settings you choose to enter, such as height, age, sex, activity level and weight goal.
  • AI estimates: when you ask the app to estimate a food, the description and any photo you add are sent to our AI provider to produce the estimate. The resulting text and numbers are saved in your log; photos are not stored by the app.
  • Monster AI chat: when you ask the in-app assistant a question, your question and the parts of your log needed to answer it are sent to our AI provider. The app keeps your recent messages, the figures the assistant looked up to answer them, and a few short notes the assistant saves about you (for example, a goal you mentioned) so later answers have context. You can start a new chat or delete those notes in the chat at any time.
  • Usage records: when you were last active, how many AI estimates you've used each day, and an estimate of their cost. These help us keep the app running within its limits.
  • Apple Health data (only if you connect it): see the next section.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools. The app stores a copy of your own data in your browser so it loads quickly; that copy stays on your device.

Apple Health data

Connecting Apple Health is optional. Monster is a web app and can’t read Apple Health itself. Instead, you add a shortcut (“Monster Health”) to Apple’s Shortcuts app on your iPhone. When it runs, it reads your Active Energy and step count from Apple Health for today and the two days before, adds each up per day, and sends Monster those daily totals with the date and your private key. If you add the optional weight step and turn on Weight from Apple Health in your profile, it also sends each day’s first body weight reading (for example from a smart scale) and the time it was taken.

  • Apple asks your permission before the shortcut can read Health data. It reads Active Energy, steps and (if you add that step) body weight only. It doesn’t read, and Monster never receives, your heart rate, sleep, individual workouts, body fat, medical records or any other Health data.
  • Active Energy can come from any device or app that writes it to Apple Health, such as an Apple Watch, WHOOP, Oura or Garmin. Monster only sees the daily totals, not which device recorded them.
  • We use the numbers only to calculate your daily calorie burn inside Monster. They are never sold, shared with other users, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • Your private key links the shortcut to your account. It’s stored only on our server, and you can replace it or disconnect at any time from the app’s Apple Health page.

How we use your information

  • To run the app: show your logs, totals, goals and trends, and keep them in sync across your devices.
  • To produce AI calorie and macro estimates, and answers in the Monster AI chat, when you ask for them.
  • To manage accounts: approve sign-ups, send password-reset emails you request, and apply daily usage limits.
  • To keep the app secure and fix problems.

Who processes your information

We rely on a few service providers to run the app. Each receives only what it needs:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions), which hosts accounts and data in the United States.
  • Anthropic (Claude API), which receives food descriptions and photos you submit for an estimate, and your chat questions with the parts of your log needed to answer them. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, API inputs are not used to train its models.
  • Apple, whose Health and Shortcuts apps on your iPhone read and send your Active Energy, steps and (optionally) weight if you connect Apple Health, as described above.
  • GitHub Pages, jsDelivr and Google, which serve the app's page, code libraries and fonts. Like any website host, they may log your IP address and browser type when the page loads.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

Who can see your data

Your logs are private to your account: other users cannot see them. The app owner can see account-level information in an owner-only admin console: your email, name, account status, sign-up and last sign-in and activity dates; AI usage counts and their estimated cost; counts and dates about your logging (days logged, how many foods and workouts, how many weigh-ins, the first and last day logged); whether Apple Health is connected, when it last synced and how many days it has sent; your app settings (daily-burn setup, what you track, your goal type, whether weight from Apple Health and resting-burn learning are on); and how much storage your data uses. The console doesn't show the contents of your logs — no foods, calories, weights or Apple Health numbers. The app's operator has technical access to the database for maintenance and only looks at your data when needed to fix a problem or when you ask for help.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules restrict each account to its own data, and Apple Health keys and admin settings are readable only by our server. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit access to what the app needs.

How long we keep it, and your choices

  • Disconnect Apple Health at any time from the app’s Apple Health page. This turns off your key and deletes the Active Energy, steps and weigh-ins we received from Apple Health. You can also delete the shortcut from the Shortcuts app, or turn off its Health access in your iPhone’s Settings.
  • Edit or delete entries in the app whenever you like.
  • Delete your account by emailing pibcury@gmail.com. We will delete your account, your logs, your chat history and any Apple Health data within 30 days.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or ask what we hold about you, by emailing the same address.

We otherwise keep your information for as long as your account exists.

Children

Monster is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If a change significantly affects how Apple Health data or other personal information is used, we'll let users know in the app before it takes effect.
