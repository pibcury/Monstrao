Monstrão Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

The short version

  • Monstrão is a small, non-commercial calorie, protein and weight tracker shared with invited friends.
  • We use your information only to run the app for you. We don't sell it, show ads, or share it for marketing.
  • If you connect WHOOP, we read only your daily calories burned. You can disconnect at any time.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and everything in it whenever you like.

Who we are

Monstrão (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a personal project operated by Pedro Cury for himself and people he invites. It is not a company product and is not offered commercially. Questions about this policy or your data can go to pibcury@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your email address and the name you sign up with. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see or store it.
  • What you log: foods and drinks (descriptions, calories, protein), calories burned, body weight readings, and profile and goal settings you choose to enter, such as height, age, sex, activity level and weight goal.
  • AI estimates: when you ask the app to estimate a food, the description and any photo you add are sent to our AI provider to produce the estimate. The resulting text and numbers are saved in your log; photos are not stored by the app.
  • Usage records: when you were last active, how many AI estimates you've used each day, and an estimate of their cost. These help us keep the app running within its limits.
  • WHOOP data (only if you connect WHOOP): see the next section.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools. The app stores a copy of your own data in your browser so it loads quickly; that copy stays on your device.

WHOOP data

Connecting WHOOP is optional. When you choose to connect it, WHOOP asks for your permission, and the app requests these permissions:

PermissionWhat we do with it
read:cyclesRead the energy expenditure (kilojoules) for each of your WHOOP days (“cycles”), plus each cycle's ID and start and end times. We convert it to calories and fill in your daily calories burned. We read up to the last 60 days when you first connect, then check for updates about every 30 minutes.
read:profileRequested so the connection identifies your WHOOP account. The app does not store your WHOOP profile details.
offlineLets the app keep syncing in the background without you signing in to WHOOP again.

We do not request or receive your recovery, sleep, strain, heart-rate, workout or body measurement data.

The access tokens WHOOP issues are stored only on our server, can't be read by the app in your browser or by other users, and are used only to fetch the data above. WHOOP data is used only to show your calories burned inside Monstrão. It is never sold, shared with other users, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

How we use your information

  • To run the app: show your logs, totals, goals and trends, and keep them in sync across your devices.
  • To produce AI calorie and protein estimates when you ask for them.
  • To manage accounts: approve sign-ups, send password-reset emails you request, and apply daily usage limits.
  • To keep the app secure and fix problems.

Who processes your information

We rely on a few service providers to run the app. Each receives only what it needs:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions), which hosts accounts and data in the United States.
  • Anthropic (Claude API), which receives food descriptions and photos you submit for an estimate. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, API inputs are not used to train its models.
  • WHOOP, only if you connect it, as described above.
  • GitHub Pages, jsDelivr and Google, which serve the app's page, code libraries and fonts. Like any website host, they may log your IP address and browser type when the page loads.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

Who can see your data

Your logs are private to your account: other users cannot see them. App administrators can see account-level information (email, name, account status, sign-up date, last activity, AI usage counts, number of days logged and whether WHOOP is connected), but the admin tools don't show the contents of your logs. The app's operator has technical access to the database for maintenance and only looks at your data when needed to fix a problem or when you ask for help.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules restrict each account to its own data, and WHOOP tokens and admin settings are readable only by our server. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit access to what the app needs.

How long we keep it, and your choices

  • Disconnect WHOOP at any time from the app's Wearables menu. This revokes the app's access on WHOOP's side and deletes the stored tokens. Calories already imported stay in your log unless you ask us to remove them. You can also revoke access from your WHOOP account.
  • Edit or delete entries in the app whenever you like.
  • Delete your account by emailing pibcury@gmail.com. We will delete your account, your logs and any WHOOP connection within 30 days.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or ask what we hold about you, by emailing the same address.

We otherwise keep your information for as long as your account exists.

Children

Monstrão is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If a change significantly affects how WHOOP data or other personal information is used, we'll let users know in the app before it takes effect.
